## 4. Key facts worth not rediscovering

- **Coverage:** with clustered, still beacons a learner hears one message per life. Beacons must be spread
  (`--beacon-spread 6`) and walking (`--beacon-walk`). Wider spread (9) and faster walks are worse.
- **Scoring:** credit food only to the last-heard, well-heard sector (`--score last-heard`); a life is eligible
  only with >= 5 sectors heard >= 25 times (`--min-sectors 5`); pass rates are over eligible lives.
- **Lifespans:** medians must be Kaplan-Meier (`w2/km.py`); lives still running at the horizon bias them low.
- **Appetite:** a sated navigator idles, which inflates "value of the code". Report it with the navigator
  fallback and with a random-move fallback.
- **A supervised probe with pooled data needs ~3,000 gradient steps** to fit the 8-way mapping. A life has ~480.
- **`TAU = 0.08`** makes token policies nearly one-hot; cross-entropy copying barely moves them.
- **Defaults that bit us:** `--mem 4` (replay sees 4 rollouts), `--young 1000` (copying stops at 1,000),
  `--juvenile` also sets the young-learning-rate decay.
- **Modal:** preempted functions restart from the start unless checkpointed. Non-preemptible costs 3x.
- **Channel:** the Nov 8 A/B kept the VQ wire over plain symbols (protocol line 2562). Do not re-propose plain
  symbols without a new criterion.
